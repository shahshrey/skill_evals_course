11. Efficiency

What does the improvement cost in tokens, money and time?

How it works

  • Take token counts from the API's usage data, including cached input; never estimate from text length
  • Compare quality delta against cost delta per arm
  • Classify: better and cheaper, a trade-off, cheaper but weaker, worse on both, or reject

Watch out for

  • Loading a skill costs extra turns, and every turn reads the whole context again
  • Cost per passing run is the fair comparison between two skills
