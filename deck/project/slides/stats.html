9. Statistics over repeated runs

Is the lift real, or noise?

How it works

  • pass@k: how often at least one of k attempts passes; pass^k: how often all k do
  • Bootstrap the paired differences to get an interval; if it straddles zero, you have not shown anything
  • A permutation test on the signs of the differences gives a p-value with no distribution assumptions

Watch out for

  • Eight pairs cannot resolve a twenty-point effect; the noise floor is roughly one over the square root of the pair count
  • One run is an anecdote

Skill evals, the eleven types

A finding that looks solid on one run is often refuted by the fifth.
